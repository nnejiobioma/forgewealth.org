Our 4-Stage Testing Methodology

Stage 1: Anonymous Procurement

We do not accept free review samples or cherry-picked VIP hosting accounts from brands. All subscriptions and hardware are acquired through standard retail and customer signup channels.

Stage 2: Stress & Benchmark Testing

For web hosts, we spin up identical test WordPress environments, load 100 concurrent virtual users via k6, and monitor TTFB from multiple geographic data centers. For devices, we measure battery drain, display nits, and thermal throttling under heavy workloads.

Stage 3: Pricing & Value Auditing

Great technology should not come with sneaky price traps. We review the fine print, renewal terms, and warranty support to ensure long-term value.

Stage 4: Continuous Re-evaluation

Hardware firmware updates and hosting server migrations occur frequently. We continually update our reviews and ratings to keep them accurate throughout 2026.
