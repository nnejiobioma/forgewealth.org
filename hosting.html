Web Hosting Buyer's Advice & Tips

Everything you need to know before locking in a hosting subscription.

1. Shared vs Cloud vs VPS

Shared hosting (like Hostinger Premium) is great for up to 50k visitors/mo. As your audience expands, moving to Cloud or VPS guarantees dedicated CPU and memory so sudden traffic spikes won't slow your site.

2. LiteSpeed vs Traditional Apache

LiteSpeed web servers handle PHP requests and caching up to 400% faster than older Apache setups. Hostinger utilizes LiteSpeed with native WordPress integration for top Google Core Web Vitals scores.

3. Look Out for Renewal Rates

Many hosting providers advertise $2.95/mo then renew at $17.99/mo. We highlight transparent renewal terms and recommend 24 to 48-month billing periods to lock in low promotional pricing for years.
